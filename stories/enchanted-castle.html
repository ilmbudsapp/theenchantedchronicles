Overview: The Enchanted Castle is a short fantasy tale from The Enchanted Chronicles by Agron Osmani. Warm light glowed from its windows The story explores wonder, mystery, and the allure of the unknown. Set in ancient castle under moonlight, it offers a atmospheric, mysterious, wondrous reading experience with low to moderate — fascination over fear.
