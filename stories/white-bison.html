Overview: White Bison is a short fantasy tale from The Enchanted Chronicles by Agron Osmani. In a small, impoverished village at the edge of an ancient forest, the people lived in constant struggle. Their crops had failed for three consecutive seasons The story explores hope, sacred nature, and community resilience. Set in struggling village and open plains, it offers a hopeful, sacred, communal reading experience with moderate to high — struggle and renewal. The tale stands alone — no prior reading required — and fits naturally within the broader Enchanted Universe. Readers who enjoy emotional fantasy with clear arcs will find this chronicle especially rewarding.

What is the main theme of this story?

Hope, sacred nature, and community resilience.

The ending reaffirms that courage and kindness leave a lasting mark on the world.
